2 8h-mParticipant Population
In section 8i you wrote that the consent form "clearly states that participation is limited to adults aged 18 or older with a confirmed or suspected PKD diagnosis. Participants self-certify eligibility by proceeding with the survey after reading these criteria. No personal health information is collected prior to enrollment, no telephone or in-person scree"

There does not appear to be anything in the consent form (section 13) which clearly state the age criterion.

We suggest that you update your survey to ask a question about participants age at the onset. If they answer they are under 18, then program the survey to end so they are not able to move forward.
Thank you for this comment. We have revised the consent form to explicitly state that participation is limited to individuals who are 18 years of age or older with a confirmed or suspected diagnosis of PKD. In addition, the survey has been updated to include an age eligibility question at the beginning of the survey. Participants who indicate they are under 18 years of age are automatically exited from the survey and cannot proceed to the study questions.

3 Privacy And Confidentiality
In section 11d you wrote " No de-identification is required as no identifiable data are collected."

Because you have open-ended questions in your survey, there is a chance that you may inadvertency collect identifiers. Please confirm you will review your answers and remove any identifiers inadvertently collected.
Thank you for this comment. Section 11d has been revised to acknowledge the possibility that participants may inadvertently include identifying information in open-ended responses. We confirm that all open-ended responses will be reviewed prior to analysis, and any identifiers inadvertently provided will be removed or redacted whenever feasible before the data are analyzed.

7 Consent
Anonymity is nearly impossible to guarantee. For this reason, the IRB recommends that you avoid using the term "anonymous" in your study materials.

Please revise your (a) consent forms, (b) recruitment materials, and (c) eProtocol, sections 2a, 8f, 8j, 9a, 9c, 11b, 11c, 11d, 13.1 (a, b, and c), and 13.4 to include terms like "confidential" or "de-identified" instead of "anonymous" or "anonymity."
Thank you for this comment. We have revised the consent form, recruitment materials, and the applicable sections of the eProtocol (Sections 2a, 8f, 8j, 9a, 9c, 11b, 11c, 11d, 13.1, and 13.4) to replace references to "anonymous" or "anonymity" with language describing the survey as confidential and designed not to intentionally collect direct identifiers. We also revised the protocol to acknowledge that participants may inadvertently provide identifying information in open-ended responses and that any such identifiers will be reviewed and removed or redacted whenever feasible prior to analysis.
